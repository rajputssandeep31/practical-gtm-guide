# Practical GTM guide for product managers

A detailed workbook for bringing a B2B SaaS offer to paying customers. It connects customer evidence to positioning, pricing, the buying process, first value and launch decisions.

## Download the template

- [Editable Word template](https://github.com/rajputssandeep31/practical-gtm-guide/raw/refs/heads/main/practical-gtm-template.docx)
- [Read the full guide on GitHub](GTM-GUIDE.md)

Download the Word file and complete your own copy. Replace the fictional example and bracketed prompts with evidence from your product. The workbook ends with a one-page GTM brief for a launch review.

## What is inside

1. Launch decision and commercial goal
2. Customer segment and buying trigger
3. Evidence and customer alternatives
4. Buyer roles and purchase path
5. Positioning and message proof
6. Offer, pricing and economics
7. Sales motion and channel bet
8. Evaluation and activation
9. Sales and partner enablement
10. Launch readiness and support
11. Measurement and experiments
12. Rollout gates and operating cadence
13. GTM activities and execution plan
14. One-page GTM brief

The decision worksheets include guidance, a connected worked example, editable fields and review conditions. The execution section includes 17 proposed activities across a 90 day plan, with outputs, owners, timing, dependencies and completion criteria, plus a reusable activity record. Metric definitions clarify the denominator and time window behind the launch measures.

The example is a fictional vendor intake workspace. Its targets and economics illustrate planning and are not benchmarks or customer results. This guide is most useful for B2B SaaS; adapt its buying roles and account measures for other products.

Maintained by Sandeep Singh Rajput. Prepared with AI assistance using freely accessible public references from Strategyzer, Stripe and Amplitude. No employer or customer information is included. Source notes are in the full guide.
